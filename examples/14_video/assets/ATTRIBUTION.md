`sample_door_cam.mpg` (MPEG-1 video, 9 s, 448x252) is a trimmed, 16:9

"live camera feed" in the curated smart-home docs demo.

- Recording: *Front Porch Theft 2012 11 17 101117.ogv* (2012), by Trevor Fiatal —
